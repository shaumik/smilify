Six callout components are available, matching Mintlify's set.

## Note

```mdx
<Note>Adds a neutral aside to the flow of the page.</Note>
```

<Note>Adds a neutral aside to the flow of the page.</Note>

## Info

<Info>
  Use `Info` for supplementary context — links work inside callouts, like the
  [Quickstart](/quickstart), and so does `inline code`.
</Info>

## Tip

<Tip>Use `Tip` for best practices and pro moves.</Tip>

## Warning

<Warning>
  Use `Warning` for anything that can bite: breaking changes, deprecations,
  destructive commands.
</Warning>

## Check

<Check>Use `Check` to confirm a successful outcome.</Check>

## Danger

<Danger>
  Use `Danger` for irreversible actions — deleting data, rotating keys others
  depend on.
</Danger>
