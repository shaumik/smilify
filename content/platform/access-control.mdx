Beyond requiring sign-in everywhere, content can be restricted by **role**.

## Restricting a group

Mark any navigation group in `docs.json`:

```json title="docs.json" {4}
{
  "group": "Admin",
  "access": "admin",
  "pages": ["admin/deployment", "admin/user-management"]
}
```

Three enforcement layers kick in automatically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigation">
    The group never renders in the sidebar for non-admins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct URLs">
    A member requesting `/admin/deployment` gets a **404**, not a 403 — the
    page's existence isn't disclosed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Results are filtered server-side per role before leaving the server.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Seeing it live

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="As admin">
    Sign in with an admin account. The **Admin** group appears at the bottom
    of the Documentation sidebar with a shield marker.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="As member">
    Sign in as `guest@echelonai.com`. No Admin group, no search hits for its
    pages, and direct links 404.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Role lives inside the signed session token, so it can't be tampered with
  client-side. Changing a user's role takes effect on their next sign-in.
</Warning>
